# The Robyn Effect — Brand Reference for the Website

Source: Brand Style Guide v1.0 and Voice & Tone Guide. Tagline: *Making everyday life a little better.*
Core rule: **Less, but better.** Editorial magazine feel, not influencer template.

## Colors
| Role | Name | HEX |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Background | Warm White | `#F7F5F0` |
| Text / ink | Ink | `#252525` |
| Neutral (borders, tints) | Stone | `#D8D2C7` |
| Primary accent | Terracotta | `#B56F55` |
| Hover / secondary accent | Clay | `#9C6B4F` |
| Seasonal / tags only | Sage | `#7C8775` |
| Captions, metadata | Warm Gray | `#6B665C` |

- Never pure black on pure white. Terracotta is an accent, never a large field.
- Terracotta is about 3.4:1 on Warm White: large text, buttons, and underlined links only.
- Links: Terracotta, underlined; hover Clay. Errors use Clay, never bright red.

## Typography
- **Logo only:** Balgin Condensed + Brittany (not on Google Fonts; use the supplied logo images).
- **Headlines:** DM Serif Display 400. H1 40–56px, H2 28–34px, line-height about 1.1.
- **Everything else:** DM Sans. Body 17–18px / 1.6 (never under 16px). H3 700 18–20px.
- **Small labels:** DM Sans 600, 12–13px, uppercase, +8% tracking.
- **Nav / metadata:** DM Sans 500, uppercase, 13–14px.
- **Pull quotes:** DM Serif Display Italic, Sage, with a Terracotta left rule.
- Formula: small DM Sans label → large DM Serif headline → DM Sans text.
- All caps only for labels, nav, and metadata.

## Layout and components
- 8px spacing grid. Max width 1200px. Reading column max 680px. Section spacing 64px or more.
- Flat design: no shadows, gradients, or pills. Radius 2–4px. 1px Stone hairlines.
- Primary button: Ink fill, Warm White text; Clay on hover. Secondary: 1.5px Ink outline.
- Series labels ("THAT'S SO ROBYN", "PULLING A ROBYN"): DM Sans uppercase, Terracotta, thin underline rule, never filled. One per post.
- Category tags: Ink on Stone tint, sharp corners.

## Site structure
- Nav (4 items): **HOME · FINDS · LIFE · OUTSIDE**. Series are filters, not nav items.
- Home: editorial hero → "Currently" strip (3–4 finds) → latest grid (max 6) → quiet Ink newsletter band (no popup) → footer (wordmark, nav, text social links).
- Article: single column, series label at top, inline product callouts (no sidebar), related reads as a text list.

## Logo files
Variants supplied in light/dark colorways: `therobyneffectlogo{ink,stone,sage,clay}.png`, `therobyneffecticon{ink,stone,sage,clay}.png`, `Reffect{ink,stone,sage,clay}.png`.
Clear space = height of the "R". Minimum width 120px horizontal, 40px compact. No shadows, stretching, or tagline fused to the logo.

## Photography
Natural directional light, real textured settings, hands mid-task, warm slightly desaturated grade. No staged flat-lays or stock polish. Alt text on every image.

## Voice (short version)
Warm first, funny second. Talk, don't broadcast. Short punchy sentences, parenthetical asides, specific details. No "elevate / curated / journey / unlock", no exclamation points or emoji clusters. Gut-check: would I say this out loud to a friend?
Setting: R&R, a 5-acre rural retreat. Husband Ryan (name used), kids in their 20s, new grandbaby; no other names. Starter-not-finisher honesty is a feature.
